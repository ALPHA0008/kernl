# Higgsfield AI — People Operations & Finance Policy
**Last updated:** January 2026 | **Owner:** Head of People Ops

---

## 1. Hiring Process

### 1.1 Engineering Roles

**Standard interview pipeline:**
1. Recruiter screen (30 mins) — culture + compensation alignment
2. Technical phone screen (60 mins) — fundamentals, problem-solving
3. Take-home assignment (4 hours max) — practical coding exercise
4. System design interview (60 mins) — senior roles only
5. Final loop: 3 interviews with team members (2 hrs total)
6. Reference checks (2 references minimum) — must be completed before offer

**Offer approval:**
- IC roles (L1–L3): Engineering Lead approves, HR sends offer
- Senior roles (L4+): Head of Engineering approves + CEO informed
- Staff and above: CEO must sign off before offer letter is sent under any circumstances
- Do not verbally extend an offer before written approval is secured — this has caused problems before

**Compensation bands:**
- Must stay within approved band for role level
- Exceptions (above band) require Head of Engineering + CFO joint approval
- Never disclose another candidate's offer to justify a counter — this is grounds for immediate PIP

### 1.2 Non-Engineering Roles

- Same pipeline structure but adapted per function (no system design round)
- Sales roles: include a mock demo or pitch exercise in the loop
- Operations roles: case study exercise replaces take-home coding
- All offer approvals: functional Head + CEO for any role above manager level

### 1.3 Contractor Engagement

- Contractors engaged for >3 months OR >$15,000 total contract value require CFO approval
- Short-term contractors (<$5,000): functional Head approval only
- All contractors must sign IP assignment agreement before starting work — no exceptions
- Do not give contractors access to production systems without Head of Engineering sign-off

## 2. Performance Management

### 2.1 Performance Improvement Plans (PIPs)

**PIP trigger criteria (any one of the following):**
- Consistent failure to meet agreed OKRs for 2+ consecutive quarters
- Repeated behavioral issues documented in writing with 2+ prior conversations
- 360 feedback indicates significant team impact concerns raised by 3+ peers
- Direct manager assessment that performance is significantly below level expectations

**PIP process:**
1. Manager drafts PIP document with HR
2. HR reviews for consistency and legal compliance before delivery
3. PIP delivered in 1:1 with manager + HR representative present
4. Standard PIP duration: 30 days (can be extended to 45 at manager discretion)
5. Weekly check-ins documented in writing throughout PIP period

**PIP outcomes:**
- Successful completion: documented, no further action, performance monitored for 90 days
- Unsuccessful completion: termination with standard notice period
- Do not start a PIP with the intent to terminate — if the decision to terminate is already made, proceed directly with termination process (legal has advised this reduces liability)

### 2.2 Termination Process

- All terminations require Head of People Ops review before execution
- Executive terminations (Director+): require Board notification within 24 hours
- Terminations during equity vesting cliff: route to legal for review first
- Last-day system access: revoked at time of termination meeting, not before
- Severance: standard is 2 weeks per year of service, minimum 4 weeks. Above-band severance requires CFO approval.

## 3. Expense & Finance Policy

### 3.1 Expense Approvals

| Amount | Approval Required |
|---|---|
| Under $500 | Direct manager |
| $500 – $2,500 | Department Head |
| $2,500 – $10,000 | CFO |
| Above $10,000 | CFO + CEO |

**Software/SaaS subscriptions:**
- Any new recurring SaaS expense (regardless of amount) requires CFO approval — even if monthly cost is under $500
- Renewals of existing approved tools: Department Head approval if cost unchanged
- If SaaS renewal increases by >20% from prior year: CFO approval required regardless of amount

**Travel:**
- Flights: book economy unless flight exceeds 6 hours (business class approved for 6h+)
- Hotels: up to $250/night in tier-1 cities (NYC, SF, London), $175/night elsewhere
- Meals: $75/day per diem when traveling, no individual receipt required under per diem
- Client entertainment above $200: Department Head pre-approval required

### 3.2 Vendor Payment

- All new vendor engagements require a signed contract before any payment is processed
- Invoices submitted without a corresponding PO number: return to vendor, do not process
- Invoices over $5,000: two-person sign-off (submitting manager + CFO)
- Payments to vendors in sanctioned countries: route to legal immediately, do not process
- Net-30 is standard. Do not agree to Net-15 or shorter terms without CFO approval.

### 3.3 Budget Overruns

- Department spending within 10% of quarterly budget: department head discretion, notify CFO
- 10–25% over budget: requires CFO approval for continued spending
- Over 25% over budget: CFO + CEO joint decision required, spending paused until approved

## 4. Equity & Compensation

### 4.1 Equity Grant Process

- New hire equity grants: approved by CEO at offer stage
- Performance grants (refreshes): reviewed annually at performance cycle
- Vesting schedule standard: 4 years, 1-year cliff, monthly vesting thereafter
- Accelerated vesting clauses: only for VP+ roles, requires Board approval

### 4.2 Compensation Review Cycle

- Annual review: January each year
- Off-cycle adjustments: allowed for retention risk only, requires Head of People Ops + CFO approval
- Market adjustments (above-band corrections): treated as off-cycle, same approval path
- Never share anyone else's compensation details with another employee — this is a firing offense

## 5. Time Off & Leave

### 5.1 PTO Policy

- Unlimited PTO in policy; in practice, minimum 15 days per year recommended
- PTO over 2 consecutive weeks: manager + HR approval required
- PTO during company blackout periods (last 2 weeks of each quarter for revenue-generating roles): requires Department Head approval

### 5.2 Medical & Parental Leave

- Medical leave: up to 12 weeks fully paid, extends to 16 weeks with doctor documentation
- Parental leave: 16 weeks fully paid for primary caregiver, 8 weeks for secondary caregiver
- Return-to-work plan: required for any leave exceeding 4 weeks, developed with manager + HR
- Benefits continuation during leave: all benefits maintained at company cost

### 5.3 Leave and Equity Vesting

- Standard medical/parental leave: vesting continues uninterrupted
- Unpaid leave beyond policy: vesting pauses after 30 days of unpaid leave — discuss with HR before taking extended unpaid leave
